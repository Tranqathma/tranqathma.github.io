Sources disagree
7

MoSPI, National Indicator Framework Progress Report 2026 gives 87 for 2022-24. WHO and UNICEF joint estimates gives 80 for 2023. The two differ by 7, and neither source discloses the other. Both are published here and no average is formed, because an average of two figures built by different methods over different periods is a third figure that no source supports.

What this means Reading of 21 September 2026

Two competent sources give India two different figures for the same indicator over overlapping years, and neither discloses the other.
